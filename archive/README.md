# Archive of the old jayniche.ca site

Reference only. Nothing in this folder is part of the built website.

## `old-jayniche-landing-page.html`

The "Jayniche" landing page (Hamilton & Niagara positioning), copied from the
`djmje/Jayniche-Landing-Page` repo (commit 643d2c7, uploaded Aug 13, 2026).

### Where its form sent leads

- **This saved copy** posts to `https://formsubmit.co/azzamx16@gmail.com`
  with the subject "New lead from Jayniche website".
- **The live site** (as of Oct 2026) sends leads through **Web3Forms**, which emails
  them to readbyjay@gmail.com with the same subject. So the version deployed on
  Cloudflare (Worker `white-darkness-0aa3`) is newer than this file.

The new site keeps Web3Forms so leads keep arriving the same way.

### What was carried over

- The calm, plain tone and the "not a call centre / reviewed by a real person" message
- The 3-step process (form → short call → review your offer)
- The FAQ answers, rewritten for Ontario and Alberta
- The short form and its consent line
- The colour palette (sage, sand, brass, cream)

### What was removed

- All Hamilton & Niagara-only positioning (title, address placeholder,
  "Local to Hamilton & Niagara", footer line)
- Bedrooms and bathrooms fields, replaced by condition, listing status and occupancy
