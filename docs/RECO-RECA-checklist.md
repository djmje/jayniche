# Questions to ask RECO and RECA about wholesaling

This is **not legal advice and not a conclusion** about what's allowed. These are the questions
to put to the regulators (and a real estate lawyer) before you scale up ads and the website.

- **Ontario:** Real Estate Council of Ontario (RECO), https://www.reco.on.ca
- **Alberta:** Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA), https://www.reca.ca

## Ask both regulators

1. I buy houses under my own name or company, and sometimes **assign the agreement of purchase
   and sale** to another buyer before closing instead of closing myself. Does doing this, or doing
   it repeatedly, count as "trading in real estate" that needs registration (RECO) or a licence (RECA)?
2. Does it matter how often I assign, or whether I advertise publicly (website, Facebook ads,
   Instagram) that "we buy houses"?
3. What do I have to **disclose to the seller** when I intend to assign the contract or resell
   at a profit? Is there wording you expect to see in the agreement?
4. Can I market an assignment, or the property under contract, to other investors before I own it?
   If so, what limits apply (no MLS, no photos, private list only)?
5. Can I **refer sellers who aren't a fit to a licensed realtor** and receive a **referral fee**?
   Who can legally pay it, and what must be disclosed to the seller?
6. Are there words I should avoid on my website and ads so I don't appear to be a brokerage or
   agent ("list", "market your home", "represent", "agent")?
7. Is my footer statement ("a private home buyer, not a real estate brokerage, and does not list or
   market homes for sale on behalf of sellers") appropriate, or would you suggest different wording?

## Ontario-specific (RECO, TRESA)

8. Under the **Trust in Real Estate Services Act, 2002 (TRESA)**, is there an exemption for a person
   buying or selling their own interest in real estate, and does assigning my interest in an APS
   fall inside it?
9. Do I need to tell sellers that I am **not registered with RECO** and they have no RECO protection
   in dealing with me?

## Alberta-specific (RECA, REA)

10. Under the **Real Estate Act**, is there an exemption for dealing in one's own property, and does
    assigning a purchase contract fall inside it?
11. Does RECA have guidance or advisories on **assignment sales or "wholesaling"** I should follow?

## Also confirm with a real estate lawyer

- Your standard purchase agreement allows assignment, and your offer and disclosure wording.
- How "we cover the seller's lawyer fees" should be worded and capped.
- Tax treatment of assignment profits (CRA treats frequent flips as business income; the
  **residential property flipping rule** and GST/HST on assignments may apply).

Keep notes of who you spoke to, when, and what they said.
