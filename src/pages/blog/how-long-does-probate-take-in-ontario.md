If you're the executor of an Ontario estate with a house in it, one of the first questions is how long it will take before you can sell. The honest answer is that the court part can be fairly quick, but the preparation around it usually takes longer.

## What "probate" means in Ontario

In Ontario, probate is formally called getting a **Certificate of Appointment of Estate Trustee**. It's a document from the Superior Court of Justice confirming that you have authority to deal with the estate's assets. For estates worth $150,000 or less, there's a simpler **Small Estate Certificate**.

When a house was in the deceased person's name alone, Ontario says the certificate should be obtained **before** anyone signs an agreement of purchase and sale.

## How long the court takes

Ontario says applications are typically processed within about **15 business days** once the court has a complete application. Missing documents, errors or unusual situations (no will, a will that's hard to read, disputes between family members) can stretch that out.

## What takes the most time

The court step is often the shortest part. Most of the time goes to:

- Finding the will and confirming it's the last one
- Valuing the estate, including the house as of the date of death
- Gathering bank, investment and debt information
- Preparing the application with a lawyer
- Paying the Estate Administration Tax deposit

Together, that often means weeks to a few months before the certificate is in hand.

## What it costs

Ontario's Estate Administration Tax is **$0 on the first $50,000** of the estate, then **$15 for every $1,000** (or part of $1,000) above that. A house is valued less any mortgage registered on it. Within 180 days of receiving the certificate, you also need to file an Estate Information Return with the Ministry of Finance.

## Can you sell the house sooner?

You can start talking to buyers and get a sense of value right away. With a buyer like us, you can agree on terms early and set the closing date for after the certificate is issued. Meanwhile you don't need to clean out, repair or stage the house.
