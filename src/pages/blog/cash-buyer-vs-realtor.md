We buy houses for cash, so you'd expect us to say a cash sale is always better. It isn't. Here's an honest comparison so you can decide for yourself.

## Price

**Listing usually gets a higher sale price**, especially for a house in good condition in a busy market. A cash buyer is taking on the repairs, the holding costs and the risk of reselling, so the offer reflects that.

The question that matters is what you **walk away with**, after all the costs.

## Costs

When you list, the costs often include:

- Agent commission
- Repairs the buyer's inspection turns up, or price reductions instead
- Staging, cleaning and any updates to make the house show well
- Carrying costs (mortgage, taxes, utilities, insurance) while it sits on the market
- Your lawyer's fees

When you sell to a cash buyer like us, there's no commission, no repairs or cleaning, and on homes we buy we cover your lawyer fees.

## Time and certainty

Listing a house that needs work can mean weeks or months on the market, and conditional offers that fall through on financing or inspection. A cash buyer doesn't need a mortgage approval, so there's no financing condition. You know the price and the closing date up front.

## Hassle

Listing means showings, open houses and keeping the place spotless. That's hard with tenants, while settling an estate, or if you've already moved. Selling direct usually means one visit.

## When to list with a realtor

- Your house is in good shape or newly built
- You have time and can wait for the right buyer
- Getting the highest possible price matters more than speed or convenience

## When a cash sale makes more sense

- The house [needs major repairs](/situations/house-needs-repairs/) or has damage
- You're settling an estate ([Ontario](/ontario/inherited-house/) or [Alberta](/alberta/inherited-house/))
- There are [tenants in Ontario](/ontario/sell-house-with-tenants/) or [in Alberta](/alberta/sell-house-with-tenants/)
- You're [behind on your mortgage](/situations/behind-on-mortgage/) and timing matters
- You need a guaranteed closing date
